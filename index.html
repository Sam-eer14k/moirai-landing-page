About MoirAI Cloud

We built MoirAI Cloud to make infrastructure simpler to run

Most teams keep one console for compute, another for the network, a third for storage and a monitoring stack on top. When something breaks, the answer is spread across all four.

Synaptix™ puts those jobs in one operating environment. The data you need is already in one place, and the AI working beside your team reads it too.

  • Get more useful compute from the hardware you already own
  • Spend less on operational overhead, idle resources and long-term infrastructure cost

Why Synaptix™

Why infrastructure teams choose Synaptix™

Scroll through the reasons. The matching layer lights up in the diagram.

Reason 1

Integrated AI operations

The AI is part of the operating layer, not a bolt-on. It sees the same state, logs and metrics your team sees, so its suggestions come with context. Your team approves what runs.

Reason 2

Unified infrastructure control

Compute, networking and storage are managed from one connected layer. You stop moving between disconnected tools, and a change in one place shows up everywhere else.

Reason 3

Open operating platform underneath

MoirAI Cloud's own operating platform sits on established infrastructure technologies. You modernize on a foundation your team can understand, with less risk than a ground-up rebuild.

Reason 4

Interoperability

It works across data centers, private cloud and hybrid infrastructure, so you can modernize one environment at a time instead of all at once.

Synaptik™ Cell

More useful compute from the servers you own

Synaptik™ Cell is our proprietary compute technology. Instead of traditional VMs it uses a lighter workload execution model, with materially lower memory overhead.

[CONFIRM: memory overhead vs VMs]

Lower workload overhead

Less memory spent on virtualization, more left for the work itself.

[CONFIRM: typical consolidation ratio]

Fewer physical servers

Less overhead per workload can mean less hardware to buy, power and cool.

[CONFIRM: workloads per host]

Higher workload density
